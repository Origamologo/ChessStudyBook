ChessStudyBook is an offline, open-source Android app designed for studying chess books alongside an interactive chessboard and PGN game viewer/editor.

Features:
- Split-screen reader supporting PDF, EPUB, and plain text (TXT) chess books while playing through variations on the board.
- Full PGN game loader, editor, and saver with support for nested variations, comments, and Standard Algebraic Notation (SAN).
- Position setup mode to quickly mirror diagrams from any book page onto the board, including turn selection and castling rights.
- UCI Chess Engine integration: automatically detects external Stockfish installations (via F-Droid or Google Play) or user-supplied local UCI engine binaries for real-time evaluation and best-line analysis.
- 100% offline with zero trackers, ads, or network permissions.
