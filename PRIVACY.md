# Privacy Policy for ChessStudyBook

**Effective Date:** November 17, 2026

**ChessStudyBook** is a free and open-source (FOSS) Android application developed by **origamologo** and distributed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3).

## 1. No Data Collection or Sharing
**ChessStudyBook does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage analytics, device identifiers, or user information.** 

* The application operates **100% offline** on your device.
* No user accounts, logins, or cloud servers are used.
* No third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics SDKs are included in the application.

## 2. Local File Access (PDF, EPUB, TXT, PGN)
When you open a book file (`.pdf`, `.epub`, `.txt`) or load/save a chess game file (`.pgn`), the application uses Android's built-in **Storage Access Framework (SAF)** system file picker:
* The application only accesses the specific file(s) you explicitly select or create.
* All file reading, rendering, and PGN saving take place strictly locally on your device.

## 3. External Chess Engines & Store Links
* **Chess Engine Integration:** ChessStudyBook can detect and run locally installed chess engines (such as Stockfish) on your device to provide offline chess analysis. No game positions or data are sent over the internet.
* **External Links:** If no chess engine is installed on your device, the application offers optional buttons that open your device's web browser or app store (such as F-Droid, Google Play, or GitHub) so you can download Stockfish. Once you leave ChessStudyBook to visit an external website or app store, their respective privacy policies apply.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Because ChessStudyBook does not collect any personal information from any user, it is safe for users of all ages and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If this Privacy Policy is updated in future versions of the application, the revised policy will be posted in this repository (`PRIVACY.md`) with an updated effective date.
